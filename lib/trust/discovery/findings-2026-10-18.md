# Due-diligence source discovery — findings (18.10.2026)

Read-only audit for the INSTITUTIONAL DUE DILIGENCE LAYER: who the developer legally is, what the public corporate record
says about that legal person, who executes the construction, and what a municipality publishes about permits and sites.
Two runs of `scripts/dd-discover.js` on GitHub runners (39114805844 `--all`, 39115194781 `--contractors,sites,insolvency`);
the Claude sandbox cannot reach gov.il or the municipal GIS. Nothing was written, no login, no anti-bot circumvention.
The ledger is `2026-10-18.json` (16 candidates); the Trust Center lists each as "audited, not read" until a sync exists.

## What was proved

1. **The company number is the only exact identifier for a legal company, and no PROPX source carries it.** The ILA lot
   files and the MoCH lottery rows print names. A developer therefore resolves to the registry by its REGISTERED NAME —
   STRONG MATCH at best (one row, identical normalized legal name), never VERIFIED.
2. **The registry's spelling differs from the tender / lottery spelling for the same company**: the quotation mark is `~`
   (בע~מ), hyphens appear ("הנגב-אחים"), dots ("י.ק."), runs of spaces. Of 7 known developers, 7/7 resolve by number and
   only 4/7 by the raw `~`-name; all 7 meet after `lib/entities/resolve.js regKey` (quotes dropped, punctuation → space,
   whitespace collapsed).
3. **The filings register is keyed by the company number and carries the only public, machine-readable CHARGE events**
   (רישום / סילוק / עדכון שעבוד with a per-company charge id and a date) beside annual-report filings, officer changes and
   breach updates — 11 to 53 events per known developer, all 2025–2026 (a rolling window). No chargee, amount or asset.
   A charge is financing collateral: CHARGE ≠ DISTRESS, and the window's start is not the start of a company's charges.
4. **The Official Receiver's company-liquidation file is keyed by the company number** (0 on some rows — unusable) with
   request / order / stay / closure dates and the Receiver's own case status. This is the only court-insolvency record
   in machine-readable form. None of the 7 known developers appears in the fast-track voluntary list.
5. **The contractors register ↔ the Ministry of Labour's active sites join exactly**: `executor_id` = `MISPAR_KABLAN`
   (25/25 in the probe), and corporate contractors carry `MISPAR_YESHUT` = the company number. A site is therefore
   evidence about a REGISTERED EXECUTING CONTRACTOR in a city — not about a developer and not about a project: the list has
   no dates, no parcel, no permit; the site name is free text (a lot number in 5 %, a block/parcel in 2 %).
6. **Tel Aviv-Yafo publishes a real permit ladder** (request → permit → construction start (dated) → completion
   certificate → occupancy) and supervised sites with `gush_chelka` and permit holders — for ONE municipality. Everything
   from it is TEL AVIV MUNICIPAL COVERAGE; the parcel key joins it to ILA tender parcels (851 Tel Aviv tenders carry parcels).
7. **Public-company financials are not reachable** without a browser session (api.tase.co.il, mayaapi.tase.co.il and
   magna.isa.gov.il: 403; the sites are SPAs over the refused APIs; no ISA / TASE dataset on data.gov.il). The registry's
   type (ישראלית חברה ציבורית — 2 of 7 known developers) is the only public-company fact PROPX can state. Financial
   resilience is therefore "PUBLIC FINANCIAL DATA NOT AVAILABLE IN CURRENT COVERAGE" for every company, private or public.
8. **No ownership graph exists in any open source read**: parent / subsidiary / SPV / guarantor links are NOT RESOLVABLE IN
   CURRENT COVERAGE and are never inferred from a shared name fragment ("אפריקה ישראל מגורים ג'י.פי", "שיכון ובינוי נדל"ן
   (פרויקטים)" are distinct companies with their own numbers).
9. **data.gov.il's WAF refuses bursts** (~100 datastore requests in a minute → HTML 403, not a CKAN error). The sync
   pages whole tables slowly and keeps slim rows; it never queries per name.

## What is NOT integrated and why

- Debt claims (2.7 M rows): no company number on the row; the case-id relation to the liquidation file is undocumented.
- Pledges register (רשם המשכונות): listed in the catalogue, fields not yet read (the companies lane was refused before it);
  `dd-discover.js --pledges` profiles it next. A pledge is collateral on movables / rights: LIEN ≠ INSOLVENCY.
- Courts / enforcement: no debtor-level dataset; the court system is a login service. SOURCE SILENCE ≠ NEGATIVE EVIDENCE —
  PROPX never says "no litigation found".
- MAYA / MAGNA: blocked; no scraping of a SPA, no header spoofing beyond the documented public header (also 403).
