# PROPX UX, accessibility and performance: 22.10.2026

This report covers workstream E of the overnight build, merged as `b88a243`. Measurements were taken in Chromium on the local
development server, at desktop (1440×900) and phone (390×844), in Hebrew and English. The raw numbers are in
`docs/UX-BASELINE-2026-10-22.json` and `docs/UX-AFTER-2026-10-22.json` (`comparison`, `changes`, `testedAndRejected`, `notChanged`).

## Before and after

The figures below are for Hebrew. English matches.

- The page itself has no duplicate ids, no positive tabindex and no keyboard trap. Drawers keep focus and close on Escape.
- Contrast: all but 5 of the remaining low-contrast texts are the deliberately dimmed sort arrows. The other 5 are the capital card's dimmed low-confidence label.
- Performance is unchanged within the noise of a shared machine, which is about 20–30 % between runs.

## What changed

- **Launchpad.**
  - "What changed here" is tile 2 and "Deal Lab" is tile 5. Both are live-only, between `live:begin` and `live:end`.
  - The tiles number themselves, so the offline build still reads 1–6.
- **Keyboard.**
  - Two skip links: to the search and to the main content.
  - The search box announces the active option, and Escape closes its list.
  - The search field and the period selector have names, and decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
- **Contrast.**
  - Tertiary text, and the status colours in the light theme, are darker.
  - New tokens `--brass-ink` and `--c2-ink` carry text. `--brass` itself is unchanged.
- **Phone.**
  - Controls are at least 40 px.
  - Labels of 8–9.5 px are raised to 10.5 px.
  - A tape figure that sat off-screen now shows.
- **Rail.** On a 1440×900 screen, 7 items (Housing through sign-out) sat below the fold and could not be reached. The rail now scrolls.

## Tried and not kept

- **`content-visibility` on the phone transaction cards.** It cut long-task time by about 40 %, but Chromium dropped the off-screen
  cards' text from the accessibility tree.
- **Deferring `lib/explain/registry.js`.** The first render reads it synchronously, and loading it takes about 26 ms.

## Left for later

- **Rendering only the visible version of the transactions.** The section is 79 % of the page's elements: 484 rows plus 484 hidden
  cards. This is the largest remaining performance win, but `renderTx` is test-gated, so it was left alone.
- **The ⓘ buttons.** They were 116 Tab stops without a focus outline, and the largest group of small phone targets at 16×16 px.
  These belong to the Explain Everything workstream.
